# Glossary - Gloria Union

Binding for every translator and reviewer. Agents propose additions in reports; only the controller edits this file.

## Rulings (asked of the user once; options and reasons in docs/decisions.md)
- Honorifics: kept, hyphenated (Ishut-san). Name order: Western (given name first).
- Romanisation source: official English names where they exist, else an earlier fan patch, else Hepburn without macrons.
- Credits: English.
- Register / flavour (default, phase 6): casual modern English, light swearing where the Japanese is rough (damn,
  hell), no British/American slang extremes; menus and help terse and plain. Sound effects and battle cries:
  transliterate the noise as English spelling (ガンガガー！ -> "Gangaga!").
- Abbreviations: full names and terms everywhere, prefer engine changes over shortening.

## Style rules
- Punctuation map: 「」 -> "...", 『』 -> '...', ・・・ -> ..., ！？ -> !?, <more>.
- Voices (grow this as the story is translated):
  - Ishut: brash, loud, good-hearted; べらんめぇ (ぜ/ねぇ/てめぇ): "ya", "ain't", "damn", short boasts.
  - Pinger: Ishut's henchman, a cat-beastman GIRL (獣人の娘, in love with Ishut; she/her), uses ボク, polite (ですよ/ますね) but dry and snarky; calls Ishut 兄貴 = "Boss".
  - Velgas: Ishut's old rival from the same hometown, rough bounty hunter, swaggering.
  - Ruru: sweet, simple girl ("Okay!", "Ishut!"); switches to a stilted machine voice for scans and refuelling
    ("High-energy reading from...", "Commencing replenishment.").
  - Elisha: sharp-tongued, bossy, "Dad" for 親父; Zazarland: big, lazy, jovial drinker, "ya".
  - Anne Cormack: sultry, teasing (ウフフ -> "Hehehe", "dear", "naughty"). Blackmore: drawling, cynical, money.
  - Locomoco: small child Witch, third person ("Locomoco's nice!"), tantrums. Minesota: pompous scholar,
    formal ("I shall punish you!"), obsessed with "manners" ("A breach of manners!"); calls Ishut's lot "you".
  - Raspberry: rookie devil cop of the Land of the Dead, っす speech -> eager, tacks on "sir" ("I found you, sir!").
  - Rogan: skeleton ex-Pirate King, pompous archaic ("Silence, girl!", "I am the PHOENIX!").
  - Pinger calls Zazarland 親っさん "Pops"; the crew call Elisha 姐さん "Sis".
  - Yggdra: earnest bounty hunter "of justice", polite, merciless to "evil", money-poor ("Hint: bounty hunting doesn't pay").
  - Pamela: だわさ -> ", dontcha know" (also "the Great Pamela-sama"). Eater: refined ですわ ("Oh my", "gentlemen").
  - Kamuru: loud hot-blooded rookie knight, ボク, "Unworthy as I am, I, Kamuru..." (不肖カミュル); calls Elisha "Master".
  - Ajo-Grafor: mad scientist, ボク, chirpy then stuttering berserk ("I'll d-d-dissect you!"); Ruru Mk-II and Gangr's
    machine lines: stilted, full stops between chunks ("Commencing. Battle."). Gangr's ガング noises keep his name: "Gagan-Gangr".
  - The Selector (crystal oracle, speaker shown as Ishut): archaic "I am the Selector... O king".
  - Eater (speaker #15 at night): a man at night ("it's how I'm built"): rough 俺 voice, "ya", "ain't", loves "fired up",
    "champion"; by day refined ですわ. Giselle: archaic queen (じゃ/のう: "I must ask you...", "Forgive me"), polite when broken.
    Enryetta: calm, polite, ruthless ("a sacrifice for peace"; "the Judge"). Velgas (cyborg, #31): same rough voice; dying
    lines broken into odd chunks.
  - Locomoco's おねえちゃん/おにいちゃん after a name: "-oneechan" ("Raspberry-oneechan"); alone: "Big Sister" / "Big Brother".
  - Raspberry's 課長: "my section chief" (lowercase).
- Groups suffixes on unit names: 〜団 (a crew/gang) -> "<Name> Crew"; 〜隊 (a squad) -> "<Name> Unit".
- Katakana doubles of kanji unit names (コウコク兵 = 皇国兵) are the same word: translate them the same or as a
  plural.
