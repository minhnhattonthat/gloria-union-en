# Synopsis (grows as the story is translated; from the script itself)

Story scripts run in file order `script/story/2032.json` on (batches b026-b071); common subs (`script/common/`,
b072-b082) hold tutorial and shared talk.

## Chapter 1 (2032-2037, translated session 8)
- **2032:** Ishut (brash small-time pirate, homemade "ship" = a raft) and his cat-beastman henchman Pinger (polite,
  snarky, ボク, にゃ) hunt the legendary Crystal, a legacy of ancient Euforia. Rival Velgas (same hometown, bounty
  hunter, sworn to beat Ishut) shows a wanted poster: the Empire has put a huge bounty on Ishut for no reason he
  knows. After the fight a capsule falls from the sky onto the beach: Ruru, a girl who "guides the holder of the Key
  of Euforia to the Crystal"; the key is Ishut's sword. Ishut is instantly smitten.
- **2033:** Anne Cormack (Charme Pirates, sultry, ウフフ) sinks the raft and comes for the bounty. Elisha, first mate
  of the famous Gloria Pirates, rescues them on her father's orders ("the Key of Euforia, the compass, the
  descendant": Zazarland knows more than he says; keep these hints vague) and recruits them: "All according to plan."
- **2034-2035:** Sea King's Temple. Elisha says only the Lukia Empire could pay such a bounty. Ruru detects the
  Crystal's Will and, in a stilted machine voice, eats walls (minerals full of Will) to refuel. Blackmore (leader of
  the Black Fang, Velgas's "big brother", cynical, all about money) beats them to the Crystal; Anne works for him
  for pay; he poisons Ruru and takes everyone prisoner. His client wanted Ruru.
- **2036-2037:** Pinger picks the cell lock. Locomoco, a child Witch who speaks of herself in the third person and
  can cast Meteor, looks for "Symphonia" and throws a tantrum. They free Zazarland, Elisha's huge, lazy, hard-drinking
  father and captain of the Gloria Pirates, who let himself stay caught. Minesota Gray, a pompous archaeologist lost
  in the ruins, attacks them for letting Ruru eat precious minerals.
