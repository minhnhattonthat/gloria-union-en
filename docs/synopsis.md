# Synopsis (grows as the story is translated; from the script itself)

Story scripts run in file order `script/story/2032.json` on (batches b026-b071); common subs (`script/common/`,
b072-b082) hold tutorial and shared talk.

## Chapter 1 (2032-2037, translated session 8)
- **2032:** Ishut (brash small-time pirate, homemade "ship" = a raft) and his cat-beastman henchman Pinger (a girl, she/her, secretly in love with him; polite,
  snarky, ボク, にゃ) hunt the legendary Crystal, a legacy of ancient Euforia. Rival Velgas (same hometown, bounty
  hunter, sworn to beat Ishut) shows a wanted poster: the Empire has put a huge bounty on Ishut for no reason he
  knows. After the fight a capsule falls from the sky onto the beach: Ruru, a girl who "guides the holder of the Key
  of Euforia to the Crystal"; the key is Ishut's sword. Ishut is instantly smitten.
- **2033:** Anne Cormack (Charme Pirates, sultry, ウフフ) sinks the raft and comes for the bounty. Elisha, first mate
  of the famous Gloria Pirates, rescues them on her father's orders ("the Key of Euforia, the compass, the
  descendant": Zazarland knows more than he says; keep these hints vague) and recruits them: "All according to plan."
- **2034-2035:** Sea King's Temple. Elisha says only the Lukia Empire could pay such a bounty. Ruru detects the
  Crystal's Will and, in a stilted machine voice, eats walls (minerals full of Will) to refuel. Blackmore (leader of
  the Black Fang, Velgas's "big brother", cynical, all about money) beats them to the Crystal; Anne works for him
  for pay; he poisons Ruru and takes everyone prisoner. His client wanted Ruru.
- **2036-2037:** Pinger picks the cell lock. Locomoco, a child Witch who speaks of herself in the third person and
  can cast Meteor, looks for "Symphonia" and throws a tantrum. They free Zazarland, Elisha's huge, lazy, hard-drinking
  father and captain of the Gloria Pirates, who let himself stay caught. Minesota Gray, a pompous archaeologist lost
  in the ruins, attacks them for letting Ruru eat precious minerals.

## Chapter 2 (2038-2042, translated session 9)
- **2038:** Velgas, jealous of Ishut all his life, fights him fair and square. Blackmore shoots through Velgas to hit
  Ishut; Velgas holds Blackmore back, tells Ishut "head for the north treasury" and is cut down (fate left open).
  The crew escapes by sea; Ishut runs back alone.
- **2039:** At the north treasury Ishut releases a Crystal: it becomes the card "Tidal Wave". Zazarland reveals the
  Gloria Pirates' aim: stop the Lukia Empire's "Euforia Project" (rule the world with the ruins' Will), which needs
  Ishut, his sword (the Key) and Ruru (the compass). Ishut joins the Gloria Pirates; Zazarland makes him his son
  ("call me Dad"). Dr. Deluxe: optional sex-change doctor (needs a letter of referral).
- **2040:** Locomoco, chased by a trading guild after her rare magic (Tracey leads them), joins under Elisha's wing.
- **2041:** Poison-swamp island. Raspberry, rookie devil from the Land of the Dead, hunts the escaped sinner Rogan, a
  skeleton Pirate King of centuries ago revived by an unknown "benefactor"; he escapes into the swamp; she joins.
  Pirate King Kidd's gravestone talks. 
- **2042:** An island sacrifices to the sea god because of a recent "prophecy"; the prophet is under the cliff.
  He is Black Fang: he raised the tidal waves with Will and sold the sacrifices. Running gag: Ishut is always
  "out of the loop".

## Chapter 3 (2043-2046, translated session 9)
- **2043:** Pirate haven full of Black Fang vassal crews. Fiebee, Undine knight of Chantier (once the City of Fair
  Waters, now slurred as the "Rotting Sea City"; at war with Lukia for centuries since its queen refused Lukia's
  proposal), fought them as a Black Fang contractor for supplies; cheated, she joins.
- **2044:** Ruru starves without Will minerals; at an ancient-Euforia town Ishut chooses saving the townsfolk from
  the Black Fang over the treasury's ruin shards; Ruru eats later anyway. The Manta = guardian of Euforia's sea.
- **2045:** Bordolese Island (everything, coffins too, kept in barrels; skeletons in them). Anne loses a treasure
  race to Elisha and runs off ("a woman can go back on her word").
- **2046:** The Sea Dogs "protect sea creatures" by killing Sea Goblins (who hoard treasure) for their loot.
